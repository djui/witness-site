Privacy

Local-first by product design.

Witness is built for private material. The default posture is simple: keep marks on the device, make export available, and make sync, location, health context, and AI optional.

No account required for the MVP.

AI is a mirror, not a guru.

Export and delete controls are part of trust.

Witness does not diagnose, treat, or replace support.

Trust architecture

Private material needs visible exits.

01

Local storage first

The core loop works without an account or cloud backend.

02

Optional context

Sync, health context, location, and AI are separate choices.

03

Exportable notes

Marks, reflections, tags, patterns, and reviews can leave the app.

04

Editable insight

Patterns can be renamed, hidden, merged, ignored, or corrected.

What it is

  • A private moment-capture system
  • A one-button witness mark
  • A friction map
  • A personal field notebook

What it is not

  • Not a mood tracker
  • Not a journal you must keep up with
  • Not a productivity score
  • Not a social network
